## How to read

- Slow: about **125–135 wpm** (roughly two words per second).
- Kitchen-table voice, not stage voice. Slightly under-energized is better.
- **Leave the pauses in the recording.** When you see `[PAUSE 2]`, stop talking and count two full seconds before the next line. Same for 2.5 and 3. If a pause feels awkwardly long, it is probably right.
- Fluffed a line? Stop, breath, restart that **whole beat** from the top of its card.
